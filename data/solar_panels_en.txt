Solar panels convert sunlight into electricity using photovoltaic cells made of silicon. When photons hit the cell they knock electrons loose, which creates a direct current. An inverter then converts the direct current into alternating current so that homes can use it. Panel output drops when temperatures are very high and when dust covers the surface, so regular cleaning improves yield. Batteries can store extra energy for use at night. In Egypt the high number of sunny days makes rooftop solar a practical option for households and small businesses.
